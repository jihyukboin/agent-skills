# Content impressions and clicks

[Source: Naver Search Advisor](https://searchadvisor.naver.com/guide/report-expose-ctr)

The content exposure and click information provided by Webmaster Tools is a performance report that shows how much the website you registered was exposed to and clicked on in Naver search results.

### Delivery criteria

#### Exposure/click calculation area in search results
In Naver search results, the area calculated for Webmaster Tools exposure/clicks is **only provided for areas related to web search**. VIEW and blog search areas, indicators included in the content information area such as companies/schools/attractions/games, etc., and search advertising areas are not included in exposure and click calculations.

#### base date
Exposure/click information only provides data from the "update base date" to the "last 90 days", and the update base date is calculated based on search data from one week ago. If you are looking at click/exposure data for the last 7 days, you are looking at data exposed in the Naver search box 2 to 3 weeks ago from the current date.

#### Information provided
* Number of clicks: The number of times content on my site was clicked in Naver search results.
* Number of impressions: The number of times my site’s content was exposed in Naver search results.
* CTR: Number of clicks divided by number of impressions (number of clicks / number of impressions * 100)

For example, when a user enters the keyword 'A' into Naver search results, your site content is exposed 100 times and 3 of them are clicked, the CTR is 3%. Therefore, content with a high CTR means it has the information searchers want.

#### data storage
Data from the base date up to 90 days prior is stored, and if a site whose ownership is confirmed is deleted, the exposure/click data for that site is also deleted. However, it is not deleted when the site owner changes.

### Impression/Click Summary
It provides exposure and click information on content by period (1 day/7 days/30 days/60 days/90 days) and is classified into three indicators as follows.

* Total recent clicks
   It is an indicator of clicks on your website by period in the web search surface area, and provides information on increase or decrease compared to the same period in the past.

   For example, if we assume that the click index is 10,000 over the past 7 days, this means that the series of steps that lead to a search user entering a specific keyword, clicking on content provided by your site in the search results displayed, and leading to an actual site visit occurred 10,000 times. In addition, the increase or decrease compared to the previous period is based on the last 7 days, so it means the number of clicks from the last 14 days to the last 7 days.

   - yes)
     Last update: 2019.10.22
     Search period: Last 7 days
     Inquiry equipment: PC + Mobile
     Click metrics: 10,000
     - explanation
     From 10.16 to 10.22 (7 days), the number of times searchers clicked and visited content provided by my site in the search results in PC and mobile environments was 10,000.
     The increase/decrease compared to the previous period is displayed by calculating the increase/decrease rate as the sum of the click indicators for the period 10.09 ~ 10.15 and the sum of the click indicators for the period 10.16 ~ 10.22.

* Recent Total Impressions
   It is an indicator of your website's exposure in the web search exposure area by period, and provides information on increase or decrease compared to the same period in the past.

   For example, assuming that the exposure index for the last 7 days is 10,000, it means the number of times your site's content was included in the search results displayed after a search user entered a specific keyword. Exposure rank can be the first or last page of search results. This indicator can be used as an indicator that your content has passed a series of processes that lead to crawler collection, indexing, matching with user keywords, and search exposure.

   - yes)
     Last update: 2019.10.22
     Search period: Last 30 days
     Inquiry equipment: Mobile
     Exposure Index: 200
     - explanation
     From September 23rd to October 22nd (30 days), 200 pieces of content provided by my site were exposed in the results when searchers searched for specific keywords in a mobile environment.
     The change compared to the previous period is displayed by calculating the increase/decrease rate as the sum of the exposure indicators for the period 08.24 ~ 09.22 and the sum of the exposure indices for the period 09.23 ~ 10.22.

#### TOP 30 search keywords and web documents
Provides information on the top 30 search keywords and URLs with the highest number of clicks by period (1 day/7 days/30 days/60 days/90 days). Through this, we can infer which content is being selected by users over a period of time.
