# Closing a website

[Source: Naver Search Advisor](https://searchadvisor.naver.com/guide/seo-basic-close)

#### Guidelines for a clean site shutdown
1. Don't let your name or brand become a bad memory.
    * Users who have visited your site remember your site name and brand.
    * If you are simply going to stop operation, please reclaim write permissions for all users except the administrator.
    * If you plan to terminate the service, please provide at least one month notice of site termination.

2. Problems with leaving the site unattended
    * The bulletin board of a site that has given up on management can easily become filled with spam.
    * When a domain expires, it is transferred to the domain reseller without being disconnected from the website. Through this, existing users visiting your site can be automatically redirected to low-quality pages filled with information that may cause discomfort, such as adult, illegal, p2p sites, or shopping malls.
    * Additionally, expired domains may be directly redirected to illegal, pornographic, gambling, or spam sites.
