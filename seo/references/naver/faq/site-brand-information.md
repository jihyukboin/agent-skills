# Site brand information

[Source: Naver Search Advisor](https://searchadvisor.naver.com/guide/faq-branding)

### Q. What is site branding?

In addition to the URL, a brand is information about the site or operator to which the website or web page belongs.

![image](https://searchadvisor-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjEwMTZfNzIg/MDAxNzkyMTUzNzc0NDUw.ywTWR-NcFED9MUR5R1eBKy1l0yPbgxNpFdRxp22w6i0g.RuHs9VoTRcIpGC58T62F2ETUw733ZcYN7OJgTteOe_sg.PNG/%EC%9B%B9%EB%A7%88%EC%8A%A4%ED%84%B0%EA%B0%80%EC%9D%B4%EB%93%9C_%EB%B8%8C%EB%9E%9C%EB%93%9C.png)

When you click, you will be redirected to the website or webpage that is believed to belong to the relevant entity.

This provides additional information about your site that may be difficult to convey through the URL alone.


### Q. How are brand areas structured?

The brand area displays names automatically collected and analyzed by the Naver crawler.

The information is automatically updated and may change at any time depending on collection status or site structure.

However, as this is an automatically configured area, incorrect or inappropriate names may be exposed.

If it is difficult for the Naver crawler to extract information, part of the site's URL may be exposed as a brand.
The link may not click.


### Q. Can I expose the information I want in the brand area?

The brand area is automatically created as an area where the robot automatically collects, analyzes and extracts.

However, there may be errors or limitations, so if it is incorrect or changes are not reflected, you can contact us through [customer service center](https://help.naver.com/service/5626/contents/12151?lang=ko&osType=COMMONOS).
