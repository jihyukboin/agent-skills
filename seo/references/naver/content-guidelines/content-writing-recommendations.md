# Content writing recommendations

[Source: Naver Search Advisor](https://searchadvisor.naver.com/guide/content-basic)

This guide provides core principles and recommendations for creating web content that delivers real value to Naver search users. Search visibility is determined by many factors, and the principles below will help you increase the quality of your content and improve the user experience.

## Site basic settings tips
 Before creating content, please make sure that the basic settings of the site itself are set correctly.


### ① My own brand URL
The more unique your site brand name is, the better search engines can relate your site to search terms. If the brand name is ambiguous or is a simple noun, it may be difficult to recognize it as a need to go to a specific site.


### ② Concise and accurate title and description
Your site's title and description should concisely express the core topic.
- Inserting popular search terms or topical keywords that are unrelated to the brand may result in a disadvantage in quality evaluation.
- Entering the same word repeatedly may be classified as suspected abuse.
- Simply adding a lot of words does not guarantee you a top search ranking.


### ③ Important information in text
It is difficult for crawlers to recognize text in images, so please write key information on the site in text as much as possible.
If it is unavoidable to create an image, please express the content as text using the alt attribute. However, excessively long character strings may be mistaken for spam, so we recommend that you write with only essential information in mind.


## 5 core principles of content writing

### ① Write based on expertise and experience
 Search users want content that contains in-depth knowledge and vivid experiences, not just a simple list of information. Visitors should be able to read this and feel like ‘this site is knowledgeable about this topic.’
In fields such as travel, restaurants, beauty, and professional services, stories from people who have experienced it themselves are much more valuable. Please share in detail your problem-solving process, failure experiences, detailed user reviews, etc. If a site is run by a government agency or an official brand, making that fact clear is the starting point of trust.
When covering technical topics, citing official data, statistics, research results, etc. appropriately increases the depth and credibility of the content.

#### ✔ Check yourself
* Does it contain the operator’s actual experience and knowledge?
* Are you providing information that is actually helpful to your readers?
* Have you copied or copied another site without permission?
* Did you appropriately cite reliable statistics, research results, and official data?


### ② Create a site identity with a consistent theme
Your site's authority and credibility increase when you focus on a specific area rather than covering too many different topics sporadically. Naver's search engine recognizes sites that consistently publish high-quality content on a specific topic as expert sources in that field.
Fill the majority of your site's content with core topics. When introducing a product or service, content that compares pros and cons in a balanced way rather than unconditional praise earns readers' trust.

#### ✔ Check yourself
* Does your core topic make up more than half of your content?
* Are you consistent enough to be recognized as an expert source in a particular field?
* When introducing a product or service, do you provide a balanced perspective that objectively compares the pros and cons?


### ③ Communicate transparently with sincerity
 Content that contains false, exaggerated, or deceptive elements is ignored by users and is penalized in quality evaluation.
Do not copy or paste posts from other sites. When quoting a news article, you must cite the source with a link to the original text. Writing rumors from unknown sources as if they were facts greatly reduces the credibility of the site.
When using AI tools, do not stop at simply creating and summarizing content, but complete it with unique content by adding the operator's experience and perspective.

#### ✔ Check yourself
* Did you copy an article from another site or include exaggerated content?
* Rather than just publishing AI-generated content, have you added a human perspective?
* When citing, did you cite the original author and provide a link to the original text?


### ④ Deliver information in an easy-to-read structure
 Even if the information is good, if the text is not structured, the content cannot be conveyed to the user in its entirety. Please be considerate of actual readers, not robots or systems, so that they can easily and quickly obtain information tailored to their purpose.
Think about what the searcher really wants to know and explain it in detail using actual examples or exact numbers. It is best to actively use titles, subheadings, paragraph breaks, and numbering, and to keep sentences short and broken.
If important information is contained only in images or videos, it is difficult for crawlers to recognize the text. It is recommended that key information be written in body text.

#### ✔ Check yourself
* Is it structured so readers can quickly find key information?
* Are the sentences not too long and structured for easy reading?
* Is important information expressed in text rather than just in images?


### ⑤ Maintain consistent management and up-to-date information
 Naver's crawler periodically checks whether the site is continuously managed. If relevant content is consistently produced and consumed by users, the Naver search engine may recognize the site as a good site.
Please keep up to date with frequently changing information, such as pricing, policies, and laws. If your site is neglected and spam content is created, you may be penalized in search visibility.
Do not use provocative titles or catchy thumbnails that are unrelated to the content in order to increase views. Please write so that readers can accurately predict the main point of the article just from the title.

#### ✔ Check yourself
* Are you up to date with changed information?
* Are you consistently publishing new content to keep your site from being neglected?
* Are you conveying the main point honestly without fishy phrases, content, or thumbnails?
